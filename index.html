The cheapest test Pilot design
  • Who: the next quarter's ~2,480 teams that reach a free limit, split 50/50.
  • What the test group gets: the forecast and the cap on the limit banner. Sampling isn't needed yet, so this is the cheap part to build.
  • Success: card-add rate moves from 40% toward 57%. With about 1,240 teams in each group, we can detect a lift of about 4 points. Our calc
  • Guardrail: small paying teams that set a cap don't spend more than 25% less.
  • Kill rule: below 44%, which is the miss case, we stop before building sampling.
